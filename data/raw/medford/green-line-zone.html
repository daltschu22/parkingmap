Green Line Zone

About

The Green Line Zone, or "G Zone," is a district-wide residential permit parking area that encompasses a roughly quarter mile radius of three GLX stops. This program replaced the old street-by-street residential permit parking system in the area as of July 1, 2024. The Traffic Commission voted on February 11, 2025 to make the G Zone permanent. Please note that to be eligible for a residential parking permits for the G Zone, you must be a Medford resident and reside within the G Zone boundary. Parking permits will not be issued for anyone who lives outside of this zone. 

What doesn't change in the G Zone:

  • All other areas of Medford outside of the zone remain street-by-street for resident permit parking.
  • All approved variances remain.
  • Your resident parking pass remains the same.
  • Your visitor passes remain the same and are only good for the street issued.
  • Enforcement values remain 24/7, 24/6, or as posted.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What does the change mean in terms of residents from outside the zone being able to park in the zone?
  • Is the City considering windshield stickers to denote which cars have permits?
  • How does the district parking help workers who come to this area?
  • Will there be a change in enforcement?
  • What does this mean for people living on a street that's marked red on the map?
  • Are there any measures the City can take to help prevent vehicles from parking too close from private driveways?
  • How will I know if a street falls within the G Zone?
  • What's the timeline for this project?

What does the change mean in terms of residents from outside the zone being able to park in the zone?

There is no change for visitors. If someone from outside the zone that wishes to visit you, you will continue to provide them with your visitor pass (to be used only for the permitted street), just as you would before the zone is implemented.

Is the City considering windshield stickers to denote which cars have permits?

At this time, the City of Medford doesn’t issue/use stickers. The City uses license plate technology; permits are associated with a license plate which corresponds to a car registered to an address on that street. In the proposed program, the street would be ‘coded’ as being within the zone. As the City moves forward with our parking program, we will continue to review our processes, including how we acknowledge participation.

How does the district parking help workers who come to this area?

With potentially improved availability for resident on-street parking, business customers/employees may have better availability in pay-to-park and other parking currently available to those users.

Will there be a change in enforcement?

No, days and times of enforcement remain 24/7, 24/6, or as posted.

What does this mean for people living on a street that's marked red on the map?

Streets that are marked red are pay-to-park from 8am to 6pm.

This is currently unrestricted overnight (unless otherwise posted), so no permit is needed for these particular streets.

If residents of these streets register their cars as garaged in Medford, they will be able to participate in the resident parking program and park anywhere within the G Zone.

Are there any measures the City can take to help prevent vehicles from parking too close from private driveways?

There are existing regulations to not park with 2-3 feet of a curb cut. The Parking Dept. or Police can issue citations for this. If a car is blocking your driveway, that is considered a safety violation and falls under the Police Department. If so, you should call the MPD non-emergency line 781-395-1212 and report it.

How will I know if a street falls within the G Zone?

Streets in the G Zone are marked in the above G Zone map. 

Parking signs in the zone will also be marked with a green "G" sticker in the bottom corner. 

What's the timeline for this project?

  • June 2022 - Private Way Community meeting 
  • July–September 2022 - Community Meeting, before GLX opening 
  • September 2022 - Kiosk installed in City Hall allowing for participation in the resident parking program and to pay tickets (before 21days) with a credit card. 
  • January 2023 - Sign Review Project begins. 
  • September 2023–January 2024 - Updated business/permit signage to be consistent. Program will continue city wide through 2024. 
  • December 2023 - Resident Parking renewal includes an option to mail visitor passes through the online portal, at a minimal (postage) cost to resident. 
  • January 2024 - Traffic Commission updates/variance are now added in a real-time database. 
  • March 2024 - Community Meeting on GLZ. 
  • April 2024 - Proposal approved by Traffic Commission on April 9, 2024. 
  • May 2024-June 2024 - G decal added to signage in the zoned area.
  • June 2024 – 3 new pass programs in the G Zone approved
  • July 1, 2024 - Program begins

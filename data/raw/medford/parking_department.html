Parking Department

  • Mission Statement

Mission Statement

The City previously outsourced parking enforcement to a third party who remitted a percentage of revenue back to the City based on the prior year collections. Based on the work of the Mayor's Commission on Parking Policy and Enforcement that completed its work in Spring 2021, the City decided to bring parking operations in-house beginning in Fiscal Year 2022.

The Parking Director is responsible for leading the department, working directly with other members of the Administration - including the Mayor's Office, Police Department, Department of Public Works, the Director of Traffic and Transportation, as well as other stakeholders - to implement and develop a new parking policy structure and operation based heavily on the Commission's findings.

Creating an Account and Login Instructions

Creating An Account and Login Instructions 

Please login or create an account to apply for or renew your parking permits:

Steps to online registration

  1. Create an account at this link
  2. Once your account is created, in the Active Permits section select “Add permit” button on right side – a popup will appear requesting vehicle information.
  3. Enter vehicle information and select “add to cart” (If a message indicates that the vehicle's plate is already in the system. Please contact the Parking Clerks to clear the old information.)
  4. Repeat process for your second vehicle
  5. Residential Parking Permit: Upload a clear copy of the Massachusetts vehicle registration (must have the City of Medford address printed by the Registry of Motor Vehicle (RMV), to apply for a Residential Parking Permit). The street address is required to be printed by the RMV in the Garaged or Owner-Mailing Address. The RMV Registration Replacement, RMV Residential Address form, RMV Renewal Letter, handwritten address changes are not acceptable. For the Proof of Address section: you may choose to upload one of the following: 1) Vehicle Registration; 2) Driver's License; 3) Lease Agreement/Deed or 4) Utility bill (each document must indicate the City of Medford address of the residence). The Residential Parking Permit only allows the vehicle to park on the street of the residence or the variance.
  6. Business Parking Permit: Upload a clear copy of the vehicle registration. For the Business Proof of Address section: you may choose to upload one of the following: 1) Letter of Employment from the business; 2) Business Card or 3) an official document pertaining to the business (each document must indicate the City of Medford address of the business). 
  7. Residential Commuter Permit: Upload a clear copy of the Massachusetts vehicle registration (must have the City of Medford address printed by the RMV, to apply for a Residential Commuter Permit). For the Proof of Address section: you may choose to upload one of the following: 1) Vehicle Registration; 2) Driver's License; 3) Lease Agreement/Deed or 4) Utility bill (each document must indicate the City of Medford address of the residence). The street address is required to be printed by the RMV in the "Garage Address" (supersedes the other addresses) or Owner-Mailing Address. The RMV Registration Replacement, RMV Residential Address form and handwritten addresses are not accepted.
  8. On the Buy Permit Screen - Permit Shopping cart details will show the vehicles you have registered with zero $ value – you must select “accept terms” and select the “Buy” button.
  9. A pop up will appear regarding privacy and terms of reference.
  10. If you agree - select yes.
  11. The registration process is complete – you will receive an email confirming account setup. An additional email will be sent when the permit(s) are approved.  

Please allow up to four business days for the determination of your permit application. Contact us if you do not receive approval or rejection within two business days. Please note you don't have the right to park until the permit is approved and paid for.

Residential Parking Permit

Residential Parking Permit

  • Various streets in the city are designated as Resident Parking Permit (RPP). If you live on a street with this designation you will need to obtain a Resident Parking Permit which will allow you to park on the street. A list of RPP streets can be found here. 
  • Resident parking permits can be obtained at this link or in-person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase). 

  • If obtaining a Residential Parking Permit in-person, bring the original physical Massachusetts Certificate of Registration for the vehicle. As a requirement it must include the unit/apartment, must be clearly indicated in the “Garaged Address”, on the valid Massachusetts certificate of registration.

    • Handwritten address changes or the RMV Change of Address Forms are not acceptable.

    • One Proof of Residency is required such as: Utility Bill (Internet, Gas, Heat, Electric and Water), Lease, Deed, Mass Driver License or Medford Tax Statement (each document must have the full City of Medford address).

  • The cost of the permit is dependent on how many parking permits have been issued to a residence : $20 each for the first two cars, 3rd is $30, 4th is $40 and 5th is $50 (5 is the maximum amount of permited vehicles per residence).

  • Resident Parking Permits are FREE for Seniors ages 65 and older (with Drivers License for proof of DOB)

    • The name on the Registration and Driver License must match.

  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Visitor Parking Permit

Visitor Parking Permit

  • Visitor parking permits are FREE for seniors 65 years and older regardless of resident parking permit status.
  • Visitor parking permits may be used no more than two days in any one-week period, except for Sundays. The visitor permits shall be used only while visitors are actually visiting the household to which the permits are issued.
  • NOTE: ONLY TWO VISITOR PASSES CAN BE ISSUED TO A HOUSEHOLD. This is done to to limit fraud and ensure that there are ample parking spaces available on resident permit parking streets. 
  • If obtaining visitor passes in-person, please bring a Massachusetts Driver's License, Massachusetts State Identification Card, or Temporary Massachusetts Driver's License with your Medford address printed by the RMV (signed on the back address changes will not be accepted for Visitor Parking Permits) and one of the following: Tax Statement, Lease Agreement, Deed, Utility Bill, or Vehicle Registration (each document must indicate the City of Medford address of the residence). 
  • Click here to see the full list of Visitor Parking Guidelines, as of December 1, 2022. With questions, contact the Traffic Commission at (781) 391-6409
  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Resident Commuter Parking Permit

Resident Commuter Parking Permit

  • Resident Commuter parking permits allow Medford residents to park in designated commuter lots or streets, in areas signed for Commuter Permit parking. 
  • Commuter parking permits can be obtained at this link or in-person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase). 
  • If obtaining a permit in-person, please bring a current valid vehicle registration with your Medford address printed by the RMV and one of the following: Massachusetts Driver's License, Deed, Lease Agreement, or Utility Bill.  
  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Business Parking Permit

Business Parking Permit

  • Business parking permits can be obtained at this link or in-person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase). 
  • Business parking permits are for Medford business employers and employees to have access to municipal parking lots and business parking areas of the city. 
  •  If obtaining a permit in-person, please bring a current valid vehicle registration and one of the following: a letter from the business, a business card, or an official business document (each document must have the Medford address of the business). 
  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Senior Parking Permit

Senior Parking Permit 

Senior residents of Medford (65+ years) are eligible for Senior Resident Parking Permits and Senior City (Meter/Municipal Lots) Parking Permits. 

Both types of Senior Parking Permits must be obtained in-person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).

All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Senior Resident Parking Permit - FREE

Senior residents of Medford (65+ years) are eligible for a free residential parking permit. 

Please bring D.O.B. identification and a clear copy of the Massachusetts vehicle registration (must have the City of Medford address printed by the Registry of Motor Vehicle (RMV), to apply for a Residential Parking Permit). The street address is required to be printed by the RMV in the "Garaged Address" if the "Owner-Mailing Address" is different. (RMV Registration Replacement, RMV Residential Address Form, RMV Renewal Letter, and handwritten address changes will not be accepted). 

Senior City (Meter/Municipal Lots) Parking Permit - $25

Senior residents of Medford (65+ years) are also eligible for a parking permit that allows the vehicle to park at the parking meters, kiosk areas, and in municipal lots without paying at the site. 

Please bring D.O.B. identification and a clear copy of the Massachusetts vehicle registration (must have the City of Medford address printed by the Registry of Motor Vehicle (RMV), to apply for a Residential Parking Permit). The street address is required to be printed by the RMV in the "Garaged Address" if the "Owner-Mailing Address" is different. (RMV Registration Replacement, RMV Residential Address Form, RMV Renewal Letter, and handwritten address changes will not be accepted). 

  • Allowed Parking with Senior Citywide Meter and Municipal Lot Parking Permit:

    • The vehicle can park at the parking meters, kiosk areas and municipal parking lots and does not have to pay at the site.
    • The vehicle can park at the kiosk machines with all-day parking located on and around Boston Avenue and College Avenue, Hillside/Tufts Area, Greenline Station.
    • The vehicle can park in the Commuter Parking Permit Lots after 10 AM
      • Playstead Road Commuter Lot on Playstead Road by the West Medford Commuter Rail Station
      • Upper City Hall Lot, Commuter Parking Lot (facing HWY 93 South)

Home Healthcare Parking Permit

Home Healthcare Parking Permit

  • Home Healthcare parking permits can be obtained in-person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase). 

  • Home Healthcare parking permits are for Medford Home Healthcare businesses and workers to have access to municipal and business lots, as well as residential parking throughout the city. 
  • When obtaining a permit in-person, please bring a current valid vehicle registration and one of the following: a letter from the business, a business card, or an official business document stating the employment of the professional.
  • All Parking Permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Landlord Permit

  • $150 per year (in-person only)
  • Only available for purchase in person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).
  • This pass is for landlords who own property within the City.
  • Requirements: Valid vehicle registration and proof of ownership (deed and/or a tax bill).
  • Permit is valid on the street of your rental property.
  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Postal Service Permit

  • $30 per year
  • Only available for purchase in person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).
  • Must provide a valid vehicle registration and a current Postal ID.
  • This permit is only applicable to U.S.P.S. employees.
  • Only valid on permitted streets, not lots or metered spaces.
  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Realtor Permit

  • $150 per year
  • Only available for purchase in person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).
  • Only valid on permitted streets, not lots or metered spaces.
  • Requirements: Valid vehicle registration and employment proof from agency (letter on company letterhead or business card).
  • All parking permits and Visitor Parking Permits are valid for a period of one calendar year (January 1 until December 31)

Residential Variance Parking Permit

  • Residents that have had their Variance Residential Parking Permit approved from the previous year may apply online for the new 2026 Residential Parking Permit and upload the required documents. 
  • All new Variance Residential Parking Permits must be obtained in person at the Parking Department Office, Room 110, Medford City Hall.

Temporary Parking Passes

  • Moving Pass
  • New Resident Pass
  • 2-Day Temporary Pass
  • 4-Day Temporary Pass
  • Two Week Temporary Rental Vehicle Transfer Notice

Moving Pass

  • $50 per use.
  • Only available for purchase in person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).
  • This permit is only good for one day.
  • Tow Zone, No Parking signs must be posted 48 hours prior to the day of moving.
  • Requirements: Printed lease agreement, deed, or utility bill in your name.

New Resident Pass

  • $10 per use.
  • Only available for purchase in person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).
  • This pass is for new residents to Medford who are in the process of transferring their vehicle registration to Medford.
  • This permit is only good for 2 weeks. It is a one-time permit.
  • Requirements: Massachusetts registration and a lease agreement or a utility bill in your name.

2-Day Temporary Pass

  • Cost: $25
  • For all permitted streets in the City.
  • These passes must be purchased in-person at the Parking Department (City Hall - Room 110) by cash or check.
  • Passes can only be bought once in a thirty day period. 
  • Passes are only valid for the street and dates that are written on them by the Parking Department. 
  • If the purchaser does not have a current resident parking pass, they must prove residency in another way, such as in the form of a tax bill with the requestor's name and/or a utility bill (phone, cable, electricity) verified by location address.
  • The two-day temporary visitor pass may be used no more than two consecutive days in a one week period.

  • As a reminder, commercial vehicles are prohibited from overnight street parking per Traffic Commission regulations. 

  • Cannot be combined with other temporary passes.

Two Week Temporary Rental Vehicle Transfer Notice

  • Free.
  • Only available in person at City Hall - Room 110 (behind the main staircase).
  • This notice serves to allow parking permit holders to temporarily transfer permits to rental replacement vehicles.
  • The Rental Two-week Temporary Transfer Notice is for valid permits.
  • This permit is only good for 2 weeks.
  • Requirements: (physical documents only, digital information will not be accepted):
    • Original certificate of registration of the permitted vehicle.
    • Original certificate of registration of the rented vehicle.
    • Rental agreement document from the company.

Some requirements can be waived depending on the account. Please contact the Parking Clerks for more information.

Handicap Parking Info

A handicap placard allows you to park in any handicap spot in the City regardless if the street is resident permit parking. 

It is important to note however that if you are unable to find an empty handicap spot on your desired street and need to park in a general parking spot, you will need a permit if that street is designated as resident permit parking. 

Resident Parking Permits are free for people 65 years of age or older. You can obtain a Resident Parking Permit by clicking here, or by visiting the Parking Department office located in Room 110 at Medford City Hall.

If obtaining a permit in-person, please bring a current valid vehicle registration with your Medford address printed by the RMV and one of the following: Massachusetts Driver's License, Deed, Lease Agreement, or Utility Bill

Ticket Payment Procedures and Methods

Tickets older than 21 days cannot be paid for online.

  • Cash payments are accepted as payment for all tickets and are processed at the window of the Parking Department, room 110 at Medford City Hall (DO NOT MAIL CASH)
  • Debit or Credit Card in the ticket portal  are accepted before the 90-day Registration Hold.
  • U.S. Postal Money Orders are accepted payment for all tickets (even with Registration Holds)
  • Checks, MoneyGram, Western Union and other Money Orders (accepted before the 90-day Registration Hold).

Tickets within the 21 days of the appeal period.

  • Online at​​​​​: Ticket Portal
  • In person cash payment at the Treasury window – in Room 108 at Medford City Hall. 
  • By check: Payments are to be made out directly to the City of Medford. 
  • By Mail: Payments may be sent to the address below within fifteen (15) days of the ticket being issued.

Pay by mail: check or money order – DO NOT MAIL CASH.

Send check or money order to: Medford City Hall, Parking Department Room 110, 85 George P. Hassett Drive Medford, MA 02155

Payments received after 15 days run the risk of incurring additional fees. A payment drop box is located inside Medford City Hall near Room 110.

If there is a hold on your registration and/or license renewal with the Registry of Motor Vehicles, known as the RMV, (state operated), the City of Medford’s Parking Department will not be able to assist with releasing the hold at this time.

Contact the City of Medford Parking Clerk at (781) 393-2463, or email: parking@medford-ma.gov, for additional information.

Registry Holds: If there is a hold on your registration and/or license renewal with the Registry of Motor Vehicles (state operated), known as the RMV, the hold will be cleared once the ticket has been paid.

Please be aware that any returned checks will incur a $25 fee on top of the violation payment.

Info on Appeals

All tickets must be appealed less than 21 days after being issued. Appeals will not be accepted after 21 days.  

Appeal a ticket online at this link.
